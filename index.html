Shipping, returns and payment

We accept prepaid orders only. After you send your order on WhatsApp, we share UPI payment details. Your order is confirmed once payment is received. Cash on delivery is not available.

Orders are dispatched after payment confirmation. Delivery time depends on your location and is shared on WhatsApp when we confirm your order.

Returns and refunds

If your item arrives damaged or incorrect, message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with photos or an unboxing video. We will arrange a replacement or refund.
